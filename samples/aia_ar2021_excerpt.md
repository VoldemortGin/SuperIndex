# AIA Group Limited — 2021 Annual Report (excerpt)

## Chairman's Statement

As the world continues to adapt to new ways of working and living, 2021 proved to be
another complex year. The life and health insurance industry plays a vital role in
helping people cope with uncertainty.

### Dividend

The Board has recommended a final dividend of 108.00 Hong Kong cents per share, which
is an increase of 8 per cent. This brings the total dividend for 2021 to 146.00 Hong
Kong cents per share.

| Component | 2021 (HK cents) | 2020 (HK cents) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interim dividend | 38.00 | 35.00 | 8.6% |
| Final dividend | 108.00 | 100.00 | 8.0% |
| **Total dividend** | **146.00** | **135.00** | **8.1%** |

### Capital Management

The Group LCSM cover ratio was 399 per cent at 31 December 2021.

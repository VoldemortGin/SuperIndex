## 主席报告 Chairman's Statement

2022 was a year that tested the resilience of every insurer in the region. Border
restrictions eased only gradually, financial markets were volatile and interest rates
rose faster than at any time in the last four decades. Against this backdrop, Harbour
Life Group continued to put its customers first, and our agency force, bank partners
and digital channels served more families than ever before.

Our strategy remains simple. We aim to be the most trusted life and health insurer in
the markets where we operate, to grow the value of new business in a disciplined way,
and to keep a strong balance sheet that allows us to invest through the cycle. The
progress we made this year across distribution, product and technology shows that the
strategy is working, and the Board is confident in the long-term outlook for the
Group.

Distribution quality is at the heart of what we do. We continued to recruit and train
agents with a focus on productivity rather than headcount, and the number of active
agents who qualified for industry recognition programmes reached a record level. Our
bancassurance partnerships were renewed on attractive terms, and we added new digital
partners to reach younger customers who prefer to buy protection online.

We also invested in our people and in the communities we serve. Colleagues across the
Group volunteered in local health programmes, and our foundation supported medical
research in several markets. These efforts are described in more detail in the
Sustainability section of this report.

### Dividends

The Board has recommended a final dividend of 96.40 Hong Kong cents per share, taking
the total ordinary dividend for the year to 132.60 Hong Kong cents per share. In
addition, to mark the thirtieth anniversary of the Group, the Board has declared a
one-off special dividend of 18.20 Hong Kong cents per share, payable together with the
final dividend. The Board will continue to follow a prudent, sustainable and
progressive dividend policy.

### Outlook

Looking ahead, we expect economic conditions to remain uncertain, but the structural
demand for protection, health and retirement savings in Asia is undiminished. With
our strong capital position, disciplined execution and dedicated people, Harbour Life
Group is well placed to create long-term value for our customers and shareholders.

## 风险管理 Risk Management

The Group's risk management framework is designed to identify, measure, monitor and
control the risks that arise from our business. The Board sets the Group's risk
appetite, and the Risk Committee oversees the framework on the Board's behalf. Each
business unit maintains its own risk register, which is reviewed every quarter by the
Group Chief Risk Officer.

Insurance risk arises from the uncertainty of mortality, morbidity, longevity and
persistency. We manage it through underwriting standards, product design, pricing
reviews and reinsurance. Experience studies are performed annually and assumptions are
updated where experience diverges from expectations.

Market risk arises mainly from the investment of policyholder and shareholder funds.
The principal exposures are to interest rates, credit spreads, equity prices and
foreign exchange. Asset-liability management aims to match the duration of assets and
liabilities in each currency, and derivative instruments are used only for hedging.

### Sensitivity analysis

At 31 December 2022, a 50 basis point decrease in interest rates would have reduced EV
Equity by US$412 million, while a 10 per cent fall in equity prices would have reduced
EV Equity by US$287 million. The impact of a 50 basis point increase in interest rates
would have been broadly symmetrical. These sensitivities assume that all other
assumptions remain unchanged.

Emerging risks, including climate change and developments in artificial intelligence, are monitored through a dedicated working group. Management information is reported monthly to the executive committee, with escalation procedures for any breach of limits. Independent assurance over the framework is provided by Group Internal Audit, which reports directly to the Audit Committee.

Liquidity risk is managed by holding a portfolio of high-quality liquid assets and by
maintaining committed credit facilities. Operational risk, including cyber risk, is
managed through controls testing, incident reporting and business continuity plans
that are tested at least once a year.

Management information is reported monthly to the executive committee, with escalation procedures for any breach of limits. Independent assurance over the framework is provided by Group Internal Audit, which reports directly to the Audit Committee. Counterparty exposures are managed within limits set by credit rating, sector and country, and are reported on a consolidated basis.

## 术语表 Glossary

- 新业务价值 VONB（Value of new business）：年内售出新保单预期产生的未来股东利润的现值。
- 年化新保费 ANP（Annualised new premium）：新业务首年保费的年化计量。
- 税后营运溢利 OPAT（Operating profit after tax）：扣除短期投资波动等项目后的溢利。
- 内涵价值权益 EV Equity：内涵价值加上商誉及其他无形资产。
- 偿付能力充足率 Solvency ratio：可用资本与所需资本之比。
- 赔付率 Claims ratio：已发生理赔与已赚保费之比。
- 代理 Agency、伙伴分销 Partnership、直销 Direct：集团的三类分销渠道。
- 特别股息 Special dividend：在一般股息以外、一次性宣派的股息。
